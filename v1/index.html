How it works

Ryan's model min-max normalizes batting and pitching statistics across the teams in the provided dataset, combines them using chosen statistical weights, then blends the two scores (55% offense and 45% pitching). The head-to-head percentages are each team's share of the two combined scores.

Important: This is an exploratory statistical model, not a trained or back-tested probability forecast. The displayed percentages are not validated win probabilities and should not be used for betting.
